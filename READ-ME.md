🗺️ Vision & Roadmap 2026-2027
Our mission is to transform CAWG-OS from a local prototype into a regional standard for Agro-Tech in Central Africa.

Consolidation & Intelligence (Launch Phase)
Edge AI Integration: Deployment of computer vision models (TinyML) on sensors to detect livestock diseases directly on the farm, without a cloud connection.

Automated RSPO Certification: Finalization of the lightweight blockchain module for palm oil, enabling smallholder farmers in Goma to access European markets.

V1.0 Stable Release: Complete documentation for self-hosting by local cooperatives.

Q3-Q4 2026 : Infrastructure Expansion (Signal Sovereignty)
Mesh Network Support: Enabling support for mesh networks (LoRa Mesh), allowing sensors to communicate between Masisi and Goma without any intermediary telecom towers.

Last Mile ISP Partnership: Signing Memoranda of Understanding with fiber optic providers to integrate CAWG-OS as a rural network management tool.

Mobile App (PWA): Launching an ultra-lightweight application running on entry-level Android phones, optimized for offline use.

2027: Regional Expansion & Total Sovereignty
Pan-African Cloud Sync: Establishing a federation of CAWG servers across the DRC, Rwanda, and Uganda for cross-border traceability.

Open-Hardware Autonomous Drone: Publishing construction plans for a CAWG-Certified delivery drone using locally available, recyclable components.

CAWG Academy: Creation of a certification program for local technicians (Universities of Goma) to maintain the infrastructure independently.

Why is this Roadmap "Fundable"?
Scalability: You demonstrate the project's technological growth (from a simple dashboard to AI and mesh).

Independence: The emphasis on open hardware and the Academy proves that you don't want to create dependencies, but rather an ecosystem.

Réalisme : Les étapes sont alignées sur les défis de connectivité réels de la région.
